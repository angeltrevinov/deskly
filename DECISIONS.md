# Decision Log (LLMs and Development)

This file documents relevant project decisions. The goal is to keep clear traceability of reasoning and human judgment.

## Mandatory rule
- Every relevant decision must include context, LLM usage (or without LLM), model output, human decision, and a rejected alternative.
- Before closing a decision, the why must be explicitly stated.
- If a suggestion is accepted without full understanding, it must be declared.
- Scope: applies to all relevant architecture, implementation, and testing decisions, not trivial changes.

## Official template (copy and paste)

###[Decision] Short title
**Context:** what problem was being solved.
**LLM usage:** what I asked and why (or "without LLM").
**Model output:** summary of what it proposed.
**My decision:** what I accepted, modified, or discarded, and by what criteria.
**Rejected alternative:** what it was and why not.
